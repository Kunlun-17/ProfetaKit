# Free simple invoice template (Excel & Google Sheets)

A clean, printable invoice. Type your details in the yellow cells, add up to 10 lines, and the amounts, subtotal, tax and total work themselves out. Save as PDF and send.

Works in Excel 2016+, Excel for Mac and Google Sheets (upload it to Drive, then **Open with → Google Sheets**).

**Licence:** free to use for your own invoices. Please don't resell it.
