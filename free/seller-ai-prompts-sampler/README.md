# 10 free AI prompts for online sellers

Fill-in-the-blank prompts for **Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Etsy, Amazon** and your own store. Replace everything in `{{double_brackets}}` with your real details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

**Start with the Store Brief.** Paste it first in every new chat and the answers will sound like your shop instead of everyone else's.

```
You are helping me run my online shop. Remember these details for everything in this chat.

Shop name: {{shop_name}}
Where I sell: {{platforms}}  (e.g. Shopee and TikTok Shop, Philippines)
What I sell: {{product_range}}
Who buys from me: {{customer}}  (age, what they care about, why they buy)
Price level: {{price_level}}  (budget / mid / premium) and typical price {{typical_price}}
What makes my shop different: {{difference}}  (e.g. handmade, ships same day, local brand, bigger sizes)
How I talk to customers: {{tone}}  (e.g. friendly and short, uses "po", light emojis / calm and professional)
Language: {{language}}  (e.g. English, Taglish, Bahasa)
Things I never say or promise: {{never}}  (e.g. no medical claims, no "100% original" unless true)

Reply "Got it" and a one-line summary of my shop. Wait for my next request.
```

## 1. Read competitor reviews for opportunities

*Product research · Use it: When you've found a product that already sells well for others.*

```
Below are reviews from a competitor's listing for {{product}}. Read them and tell me:
1. The top 5 complaints, with how often each comes up
2. The top 5 things buyers love
3. Questions buyers seem to have before they buy
4. Three changes I could make to my version (product, packaging or listing) that would beat this listing
5. Words and phrases buyers use that I should reuse in my title and description

Reviews:
{{paste_reviews}}
```

> **Tip:** Copy 30–100 reviews, especially the 2- and 3-star ones. Those are where the money is.

## 2. Check if a product is worth it

*Product research · Use it: Before you order stock.*

```
Help me decide if this product is worth selling.
Product: {{product}}
My cost per unit including shipping to me: {{unit_cost}}
Price competitors sell at: {{competitor_price}}
Platform fees roughly: {{fee_percent}} of the sale
Shipping I cover per order: {{shipping_cost}}
Minimum order from supplier: {{moq}} units

Work out my profit per sale and margin at the competitor price, and at 10% below it. Tell me how many units I need to sell to earn back the minimum order. Then list the 3 biggest risks and one way to test demand before ordering the full amount.
```

> **Tip:** Do the maths here before you get excited. A product that looks great can earn almost nothing after fees and shipping.

## 3. Write 5 titles

*Titles and keywords · Use it: For a new listing or one that isn't getting clicks.*

```
Write 5 product titles for {{platform}} for this product:
{{product_details}}
Rules:
- maximum {{max_title_length}} characters (count them and show the count)
- most important search words in the first 40 characters
- include {{must_include}} (e.g. size, pack quantity)
- no ALL CAPS, no repeated words, no claims I can't prove
- read naturally to a person, not like a list of keywords
Label each one with what it focuses on (search, benefit, gift, etc.).
```

## 4. Full product description

*Descriptions and bullet points · Use it: Every new listing.*

```
Write a product description for {{platform}}.
Product: {{product_details}}
Buyer: {{customer}}
Main reason they buy: {{main_reason}}

Structure:
1. Two-sentence opening that names the problem or moment and how this product fits it
2. 5 bullet points: each starts with the benefit, then the feature that delivers it
3. What's in the box / pack
4. Size, material and care details (only what I've given you)
5. One short line answering the most common doubt: {{common_doubt}}
Keep it under {{word_limit}} words. Use my tone from the Store Brief. No made-up facts.
```

> **Tip:** If the AI adds a feature you didn't mention, delete it. Wrong details cause returns and bad reviews.

## 5. Photo shot list

*Photos and video · Use it: Before any photo shoot, even a phone one.*

```
Plan the photos for my {{product}} listing on {{platform}}. I'm shooting with {{camera}} at {{location}}.
Give me a shot list of 8 images in order: what's in each shot, the angle, the background, props, and the one thing that image needs to prove to the buyer (size, quality, how it's used, what's included). Mark which should be the main image and why.
```

> **Tip:** Shot 2 or 3 should almost always show size, with a hand or a common object next to the product.

## 6. Build my reply library

*Customer chat replies · Use it: Do this once; saves hours every week.*

```
Using my Store Brief, write short chat replies for these situations. Each under 50 words, in my tone and language:
1. "Is this available?"
2. "Can you give a discount?"
3. "When will it arrive?"
4. "Is this original/authentic?"
5. Asking for a size or colour I don't have
6. "Can you ship today?"
7. Asking about cash on delivery / payment options
8. Thanking them after an order
9. Order delayed by the courier
10. Customer hasn't replied after asking a question
Put each with a short label so I can save them as quick replies.
```

> **Tip:** Save these in your platform's quick-reply or auto-reply feature so you answer in seconds.

## 7. Reply to an angry customer

*Customer chat replies · Use it: When a message makes your heart rate jump.*

```
A customer sent this message:
"{{customer_message}}"
What actually happened: {{what_happened}}
What I can offer: {{options}}  (e.g. replacement, partial refund, voucher)
Write a reply that stays calm, takes responsibility for what was our fault (and only that), offers a clear next step, and doesn't sound like a script. Under 80 words. Then give a second, more formal version.
```

> **Tip:** Wait ten minutes before you send anything. Then send the calm version.

## 8. Reply to a bad review

*Reviews · Use it: Public replies future buyers will read.*

```
Bad review on my {{product}} listing:
"{{review}}"
What really happened: {{facts}}
Write a public reply that future buyers will read. Calm, specific, no arguing, no blaming the customer, shows what we did or will do to fix it. Under 70 words. Then a private chat message to the customer offering to make it right.
```

> **Tip:** Write the public reply for the next buyer, not for the person who left the review.

## 9. Full livestream run-sheet

*Livestreams and short videos · Use it: Before any live selling session.*

```
Plan a {{minutes}}-minute livestream on {{platform}} selling {{products}}.
Give me a run-sheet with timestamps: opening hook, product segments (show, explain, demo, offer), engagement moments (questions to ask viewers, mini games, pinned comments), flash deals and when to drop them, and a closing. For each product segment include 3 talking points and one live demo idea. My tone: {{tone}}.
```

> **Tip:** Print it or keep it on a second screen. The plan is what keeps you talking when the comments go quiet.

## 10. Weekly review

*Operations · Use it: Every Monday, 10 minutes.*

```
Here are my numbers for last week: {{paste_numbers}}  (orders, sales, top products, ad spend, returns, ratings).
Tell me in plain words: what went well, what needs attention, and the 3 most useful things to do this week. Keep it under 150 words.
```


> AI can invent details. Check every fact before you post it.
