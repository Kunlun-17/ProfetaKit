# 8 free cold email & follow-up templates for freelancers

Short, plain templates that sound like a person wrote them. Replace everything in `[brackets]` with something true and specific, read it out loud once, and send.

**The one rule that matters:** every cold email needs one line that could only be written to that person. If you can't find one, don't send it yet.

## 1. The specific observation

*Cold emails · Your default cold email.*

**Subject:** Quick idea for [their company]'s [page/product]

```
Hi [first name],

I was on [their site/app] looking at [specific page or feature] and noticed [specific, honest observation: e.g. the pricing page doesn't say what's in each plan on mobile].

I'm a [your role] who helps [type of company] with [the outcome you deliver]. A small change like [one concrete fix] usually [plain-language result, e.g. means fewer "which plan is right for me?" emails].

Worth a 15-minute call next week to show you what I'd change?

[Your name]
[One-line who you are + link to portfolio]
```

> **Why it works:** The observation is the whole email. Spend 10 minutes finding a real one.

## 2. The quick win

*Cold emails · When you can show value right away.*

**Subject:** Made you something for [company]

```
Hi [first name],

I put together [a quick mockup / 3 headline options / a short audit] for [their page or product] — here it is: [link].

No strings attached. If it's useful and you'd like more like it, I do this for [type of client] as [your service].

Either way, hope it helps.

[Your name]
```

> **Why it works:** Keep the free sample small: something you can make in under an hour.

## 3. The short one

*Cold emails · When the person is very senior or very busy.*

**Subject:** [Outcome] for [company]?

```
Hi [first name],

I help [type of company] [get outcome] by [method]. [One proof point.]

Open to a 15-minute chat?

[Your name]
```

> **Why it works:** Under 50 words. Senior people read on their phone between meetings.

## 4. Follow-up 1 (3–4 days later)

*Follow-ups · Reply in the same thread.*

**Subject:** Re: [original subject]

```
Hi [first name], just bringing this back to the top of your inbox in case it got buried. Happy to send a short example first if that's easier than a call.

[Your name]
```

## 5. Follow-up 3 (two weeks later) — the polite close

*Follow-ups · The last one. Often gets the most replies.*

**Subject:** Should I close this?

```
Hi [first name],

I haven't heard back, so I'm guessing this isn't a priority right now, which is completely fine.

I'll close this on my side. If anything changes, I'm at this address.

All the best with [their project],
[Your name]
```

> **Why it works:** Giving people an easy way out often gets the reply you've been waiting for.

## 6. First message after connecting

*LinkedIn and DMs · Only after they accept.*

```
Thanks for connecting, [first name]. Quick question: how are you handling [specific task] at [company] at the moment? I've been helping [similar companies] with that and I'm curious how others do it.
```

> **Why it works:** Ask a question you actually want answered. It starts a conversation instead of a pitch.

## 7. Invoice email

*Money messages · Every invoice.*

**Subject:** Invoice [number] — [project] — due [date]

```
Hi [first name],

Attached is invoice [number] for [project/period]: [amount], due [date].

Payment details are on the invoice. Let me know if your accounts team needs anything else from me.

Thanks!
[Your name]
```

## 8. Overdue — 1 week

*Money messages · Polite, assumes a mistake.*

**Subject:** Invoice [number] is now overdue

```
Hi [first name],

Invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [date] and I haven't seen it come through yet. It may have been missed, so I've attached it again.

Could you let me know when it's scheduled for payment?

Thanks,
[Your name]
```
