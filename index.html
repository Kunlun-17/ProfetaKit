Questions before you buy

How do I get my files?

Right after payment you're taken to your download page. Save the link: you can come back to it anytime.

Which apps do I need?

Spreadsheets work in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer. Resume templates open in Word or Google Docs. The dashboard template is plain HTML that runs in any browser. Each product page lists the exact formats.

What payment methods can I use?

GCash, Maya, GrabPay, and Visa or Mastercard credit and debit cards, including cards issued outside the Philippines.

Why does my statement say Dafreta Networks?

ProfetaKit is run by Dafreta Networks, so that's the name your bank or e-wallet shows for the charge.

Can I get a refund?

Because files are delivered instantly, sales are final. If a file doesn't open or something is broken, message us and we'll fix it or refund you.
